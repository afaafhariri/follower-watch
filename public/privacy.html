Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

FollowerWatch shows which Instagram accounts you follow that don't follow you back, using the data export you download from Instagram. Everything happens in your browser. Your export is never uploaded, and there is no account or sign-in.

Your Instagram export

When you select your ZIP file, your browser opens it and works out the result on your own device. The file is not sent to FollowerWatch or anyone else. Only the "Followers and following" part of the export is read.

You don't have to take our word for it: the site tells your browser to block every outgoing data request (a Content-Security-Policy with connect-src 'none'), so it can't upload your file even by mistake. You can also watch your browser's network tab while you use it.

What's saved, and where

So you can see your last result when you come back, it's saved in your browser's storage on this device. It contains the usernames and profile links in your result, your follower and following counts, and recent follow requests and unfollows if your export includes them. It never leaves your device.

Remove it any time with Clear saved result in the app, or by clearing this site's data in your browser. Your light or dark theme choice is also kept in your browser.

What FollowerWatch collects

Nothing. There's no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Like any website, the hosting provider that serves the site's files may keep standard request logs (such as IP address and time) to run and secure the service. The site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the same kind of request information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Questions can be raised on GitHub.

FollowerWatch is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
