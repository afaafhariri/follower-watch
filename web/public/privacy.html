Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

FollowerWatch shows which Instagram accounts you follow that don't follow you back, using the data export you download from Instagram. This page explains what the app collects, what it keeps, and how to remove it.

Signing in with Google

You sign in with your Google account. FollowerWatch receives only your name, email address and profile picture. They are kept in a signed cookie in your browser for up to 24 hours so you stay signed in, and are not stored on the server. FollowerWatch never sees your Google password and cannot access anything else in your Google account.

Your Instagram export

The ZIP file you upload is read in memory to work out the result, then discarded. The file itself is never saved. Only the "Followers and following" part of the export is used.

Saved results

So you can see your last analysis when you come back, the result is saved for up to 30 days. It contains:

  • the usernames and profile links of accounts that don't follow you back
  • your follower and following counts
  • recent follow requests, pending requests and recently unfollowed accounts, if your export includes them

The saved result is linked to a one-way hash of your email address, not the address itself. You can delete it at any time with the delete button next to your result. Otherwise it expires automatically after 30 days.

Technical data

  • Your IP address is kept in server memory for a few minutes to limit how many requests one person can send.
  • The hosting provider records standard request logs (such as IP address, time and page requested) to run and secure the service.
  • Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage.

FollowerWatch has no ads and no analytics or tracking, and never sells or shares your data.

Services used

  • Google Sign-In to verify who you are.
  • Google Cloud Run to host the app.
  • Upstash to store saved results.
  • Google Fonts to load the site's fonts.

Removing your data

Delete your saved result with the delete button in the app, or sign out to remove the sign-in cookie. You can also revoke FollowerWatch's access in your Google account settings.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Questions or requests can be raised on GitHub.

FollowerWatch is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
